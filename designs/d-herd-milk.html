Fresh from the herd. Fill your own.

Milk and milkshakes from our cows in Antrobus, poured into glass bottles at our self-serve hut.

See the shakesHow it works

Fill. Pay. Go.

Our milk hut is open all day and needs no queue. It takes about a minute.

Fill

Pick up a glass bottle from the hut, or bring your own back. Pop it under the tap and press the button.

Pay

Tap your card or phone on the reader. No cash needed.

Go

Cap it, take it home and keep it in the fridge. Bring the bottle back next time.

Glass bottles, again and again

Buy a Herd Milk bottle once, then refill it every visit. Rinse it at home and bring it back; there is nothing to throw away.

Example prices: glass bottle £2, one litre of milk £1.60. To be confirmed.

Design idea by Pete Owen Design, based on the idea that the Antrobus Herd might sell its own milk and milkshakes from a self-serve vending machine. We do not know yet that it does. Herd Milk is a working name. Every price, flavour, opening time, the machine's location and how it works are examples, to be confirmed.
